AT&T said in July 2024 that records of calls and texts from May to October 2022 were illegally downloaded from a workspace on a third-party cloud platform. The records show which numbers customers contacted, not the content of calls or texts, and do not include names, but names can often be looked up from phone numbers.

In March 2024 AT&T confirmed that a data set found on the dark web contained AT&T-specific information, believed to be from 2019 or earlier. AT&T said it had not yet determined whether the data came from AT&T or one of its vendors, and reset passcodes for affected current customers.
