Hackers broke into the dating site in July 2015 and published profile, account and billing details of more than 36 million users a month later. Some had paid for a ‘full delete’ of their profile that didn’t fully remove their data. In 2016 the operators settled with the US Federal Trade Commission and 13 states, agreeing to pay $1.6 million.
