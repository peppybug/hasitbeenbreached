Hackers broke into the custom merchandise site in February 2019 and stole account details, including Social Security numbers that were stored in plain text. The US Federal Trade Commission said the company covered up the breach and kept its security weak. In 2022 it ordered the former owner to pay $500,000 for victims and required stronger security.
