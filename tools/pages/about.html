About Has it been Breached?

We help people look at publicly documented breach information about a company before deciding whether to share their personal data with it.

Why this exists

Companies ask for names, emails, phone numbers and more all the time. It’s hard to know whether a company has had a data breach before. Has it been Breached? puts that history in one place, in plain language, so it’s one more thing you can check when you decide what to share.

What you can do

Search a company

Look up a company by name or website.

See what’s on record

Check the date, how many people were affected, and the kinds of information exposed.

Follow the source

Every record links to the public source it was based on, so you can read it yourself.

What this site is not

  • Not complete. The database doesn’t cover every company or every breach. No result doesn’t mean a company has never had a breach.
  • Not a guarantee. Records are based on public sources and can be wrong, out of date or disputed. See Data sources for how records are handled and corrected.
  • Not a security rating. A record about the past says nothing certain about how a company protects data today.
  • Not advice. It’s information to help you decide. It isn’t legal, financial or security advice.
  • Not a personal breach checker. We look up companies, not people, and never ask for your email or passwords.
