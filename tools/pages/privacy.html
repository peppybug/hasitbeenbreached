Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Short version: we don’t ask for your personal details, we don’t use cookies or tracking, and your searches never leave your browser.

Who is responsible

This site is run by OBJ, the Netherlands. You can reach us at contact@hasitbeenbreached.com.

Your searches

When you search, the whole database is downloaded to your browser and the search happens there. What you type is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we don’t store it. If you share a search link, the search text is part of that link.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts or sign-up, and no payments on this site
  • No cookies
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts
  • No selling or sharing of personal data

Services the site uses

Like every website, loading a page means your browser contacts the servers that deliver it. Those services see technical information such as your IP address and browser type:

The fonts are hosted on this site too, so loading a page doesn’t contact Google or any other third party.

Browser extension

The Has it been Breached? browser extension shows whether the website you’re visiting is in our database. To do that it needs to know which site is open in your current tab. That check happens entirely inside your browser: the extension compares the site’s address with a copy of our public company list stored on your device. Your browsing history and the sites you visit are never sent to us or anyone else.

  • Once a day the extension downloads the public company list from hasitbeenbreached.com. Like any website visit, Cloudflare, which hosts the site, sees the request and your IP address. The request contains nothing about the sites you visit.
  • The extension stores only that company list, in your browser. It has no account, no analytics and no ads.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.

If you donate

Donations go through Ko-fi, and payments are processed by Ko-fi’s payment partners such as Stripe and PayPal. Our site only links to Ko-fi; it doesn’t load any Ko-fi scripts. When you donate, Ko-fi handles your payment details under its own privacy policy. We never see your card details. Ko-fi may share your name, email address and any message you leave with us, and we use these only to thank you or answer you.

If you email us

We use your email address and message only to answer you and, for corrections, to fix the record. We keep the email as long as needed for that and then delete it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, and object to how we use it. Contact us to do so. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, such as the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens in the Netherlands.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top.
