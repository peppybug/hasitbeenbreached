Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Short version: we don’t ask for your personal details, we don’t use cookies or tracking, and your searches never leave your browser.

Who is responsible

This site is run by [name of the person or business, and country, to be added before launch]. You can reach us through the contact page.

Your searches

When you search, the whole database is downloaded to your browser and the search happens there. What you type is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we don’t store it. If you share a search link, the search text is part of that link.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts, sign-up or payments
  • No cookies
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts
  • No selling or sharing of personal data

If you email us

We use your email address and message only to answer you and, for corrections, to fix the record. We keep the email as long as needed for that and then delete it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, and object to how we use it. Contact us to do so. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, such as the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens in the Netherlands.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top.
