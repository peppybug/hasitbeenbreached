    Questions

    Straight answers.

    Does this cover every company and every breach?

    No. The database only holds the records that have been added by hand, and it is still small. Finding nothing doesn’t mean a company has never had a breach.

    Where do records come from?

    From public sources, mostly decisions and announcements by data protection regulators and statements from the companies themselves. Every record links to its source. Read more on the data sources page.

    What’s the difference between confirmed and unverified?

    Confirmed means the linked source, such as a regulator or the company, says the incident happened. An unverified report has been reported but not confirmed by a source we could point to, so treat it with care.

    Does a breach mean I shouldn’t use a company?

    Not necessarily. A record is about the past and says nothing certain about how a company protects data today. It’s one more thing to weigh when you decide what to share.

    Can I check whether my own email was in a breach?

    Not here. This site looks up companies, not people, and never asks for your personal details.

    How do I report a mistake?

    Use the contact page. Include what looks wrong and a public source if you have one.
